Capstone Proposal

My capstone project explores a hopeful future through an interactive speculative dining experience.

Project Topic

For my capstone project, I want to create an interactive speculative dining experience set inside a futuristic greenhouse restaurant located within a future garden city. A lot of projects about the future focus on dystopian ideas or technology taking over everyday life, but I'm more interested in imagining a future that feels peaceful, sustainable, and emotionally comforting. The restaurant would combine nature and technology through things like glowing bioluminescent plants, floating tea service, vertical farming systems, climate-adaptive ingredients, and architecture integrated with gardens and water systems. I want the experience to feel atmospheric and immersive, almost like stepping into another world through food and environment.

Design Lenses

The main lenses my project would rely on are speculative design, utopian narratives, and challenging current ideas about modern cities, food systems, and sustainability. I also want to explore how different cultures design around nature and community spaces, especially inspirations from Asian gardens, architecture, and tea houses, African communal gathering spaces, European greenhouse and café culture, and Indigenous environmental practices. I want to imagine a city centered around slower living, connection, and environmental harmony.

Experience + Audience

Some features I imagine including are an interactive menu, animated environments, changing lighting and weather, and clickable ingredients or dishes that reveal how food is grown and prepared within the city. I also want users to be able to explore different areas of the restaurant and greenhouse spaces while learning about the systems that support the city. I'm interested in using JavaScript libraries like GSAP and ScrollTrigger to create smooth animations and immersive transitions throughout the experience. My target audience is mainly Gen Z students and young creatives who are interested in sustainability, culture, design, and imagining more hopeful futures. More specifically, I want to create an experience for people who feel overwhelmed by modern city life and want to imagine healthier and more beautiful alternatives for the future.

Overall, I want this project to feel like a calm, nature-centered vision of the future instead of another warning about what could go wrong.
