Capstone Research

Usability Testing Results

I observed users testing my future sustainability dining experience without explaining the project first. After testing, I asked questions about clarity, interaction, visuals, and overall impact.

Testing Overview

The usability test focused on whether users could understand the story, navigate the scroll experience, and interact with the meal-building section at the end. Overall, testers described the experience as clean, modern, easy to follow, and visually engaging.

01

The visual style felt successful.

Testers said the project looked nice, clean, and modern. The animations, text hierarchy, and gradients helped the experience feel polished and easy to move through.

02

The interaction was mostly clear.

Users understood that they were supposed to scroll through the story and read the content. The opening scroll instruction helped guide them, and the meal-building section stood out as the most memorable part.

03

Some non-clickable areas looked interactive.

One tester mentioned that a few parts looked like they could be clicked even though they were not interactive. This means I need to make clickable and non-clickable elements feel more visually distinct.

What Needs Improvement

The beginning section needs to be more concise so users can get into the experience faster. I also need to check grammar and make sure the writing feels clear, natural, and intentional. The biggest interaction issue is making sure users can tell what is clickable, draggable, or just decorative.

Did Users Understand the Project?

Yes. Users were able to figure out the navigation and understood the overall sustainability message. The Davis sustainability narrative came through, and users responded positively to the idea of creating their own meal at the end.

Update Plan

  • Shorten the intro text so the story starts faster.
  • Fix small grammar issues throughout the page.
  • Make clickable and draggable items visually clearer.
  • Keep the gradients, clean hierarchy, and animations because testers responded well to them.
  • Polish the meal-building section since it was the most memorable part of the experience.
